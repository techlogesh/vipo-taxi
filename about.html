About VAIPO

Travel should feel simple. We’re here to help.

VAIPO TAXI SERVICE provides taxi booking support for outstation trips, airport transfers, hill station journeys, one-way drops and round trips. We serve individuals, families and business travellers with a focus on comfort, convenience and clear communication.

Based in Manjini near Attur, Salem District, we help customers plan journeys across Tamil Nadu and other states. Share your travel details with us so we can discuss a suitable vehicle and trip fare.
